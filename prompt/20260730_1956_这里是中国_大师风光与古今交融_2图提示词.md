# 这里是中国 · 大师级七维与生图提示词 [新中式工笔风光 / 古今交融大片]

## 全局视觉概念 (Global Visual Concept)
- **主题**: “这里是中国” (This is China) —— 融汇华夏壮丽山河、千百年历史底蕴与现代东方文明的宏伟景象。

---

## 画面 01 · 华夏山河与千年风骨 (Majestic Heritage & Misty Peaks)

- **七维基因对齐**：
  - **【主体】**: 伫立于黄山/桂林喀斯特峰林云海之巅的红墙飞檐古阁，远景蜿蜒长城
  - **【媒介】**: 大师级中画幅风光摄影 (Hasselblad 80mm) 融合新中式绢本水墨意境
  - **【环境】**: 晨曦云海穿梭、峭壁松柏、近景朱红秋枫与古老石阶
  - **【灯光】**: 黄金时刻 (Golden Hour)、柔和穿透性丁达尔光 (Volumetric Light Rays)
  - **【颜色】**: 朱砂红、帝王金、水墨翠绿、黛蓝与晨曦暖金
  - **【情绪】**: 宏伟壮丽、沧桑深远、宁静自豪
  - **【构图】**: 16:9 极宽幅全景、景深层次丰富、满幅留白

```text
A breathtaking masterwork landscape photograph representing "This is China" (这里是中国). A wide panoramic scene harmoniously blending ancient Chinese architectural pagodas atop misty, pine-clad karst mountain peaks surrounded by a vast sea of rolling white clouds. In the distance, the iconic Great Wall snakes along mountain ridges beneath a radiant golden sunrise. Ethereal volumetric sunlight rays pierce through the misty mountain air, illuminating vibrant crimson autumn maple leaves and ancient weathered stone steps in the foreground. Elegant traditional Chinese calligraphy typography "这里是中国" integrated gracefully into the sky background. Cinematic lighting, rich color palette of cinnabar red, imperial gold, deep indigo blue, and misty emerald, shot on Hasselblad medium format camera, 8k resolution, majestic atmosphere, award-winning composition, no borders, borderless, no frame, full bleed image, --ar 16:9
```

## 画面 02 · 古今交融与东方巨龙 (Modern Heritage Horizon)

- **七维基因对齐**：
  - **【主体】**: 江面上行驶的传统红色木帆船与对岸璀璨的现代摩天大楼天际线
  - **【媒介】**: 现代人文纪实风光摄影 (35mm Cinematic Film)
  - **【环境】**: 华灯初上的江面、微雨后的倒影、霓虹与江雾缠绕
  - **【灯光】**: 暖色红灯笼光与现代霓虹流光交相辉映
  - **【颜色】**: 青橙对比色 (Teal & Amber Gold)、霓虹紫
  - **【情绪】**: 包容并蓄、古今交响、繁华自信
  - **【构图】**: 低角度仰拍、江面倒影延伸

```text
A stunning modern photography poster titled "This is China" (这里是中国). A captivating contrast between ancient traditions and futuristic modernity: a classic wooden Chinese sailboat with crimson sails gliding smoothly across a wide river at twilight, while across the water stands a magnificent, illuminated futuristic skyline with soaring skyscrapers enveloped in soft purple and golden evening mist. Vibrant neon and traditional lantern light reflections sparkle on the gentle water ripples. Modern Chinese typography "这里是中国" rendered in sleek calligraphic brushwork.
```
