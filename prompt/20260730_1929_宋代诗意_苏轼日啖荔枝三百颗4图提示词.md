- **诗意典源**：*“罗浮山下四时春，卢橘杨梅次第新。日啖荔枝三百颗，不辞长作岭南人。”* — 宋·苏轼  
- **自适应匹配流派**：【16 宋明工笔绢本】与【06 高级概念海报】自适应融合。
- **角色一致性锚点**: `[Su Shi Anchor]: Su Shi, famous Song Dynasty male poet in his 50s, refined scholar face, wispy dark goatee beard, gentle humorous eyes, wearing a loose cyan silk scholar robe`

## 镜头 01 · 罗浮山下四时春 (岭南荔园)
`A masterpiece Song Dynasty Gongbi silk painting of Su Shi, famous Song Dynasty male poet in his 50s with a gentle humorous smile and wispy beard, wearing a loose cyan silk scholar robe. He sits under a lush green Lingnan banyan tree beside a bamboo table. In his hands, he holds a branch of bright red, plump lychees freshly picked. Dappled golden afternoon sunbeams filtering through leaves, celadon tea cups. Serene scholar joy, masterpiece composition, no borders, borderless, no frame, full bleed image, 16:9 aspect ratio.`
