## 🛠️ 参数配置与负向提示词 (Parameters & Negative Prompts)

### 1. 推荐生图参数
- **宽高比 (Aspect Ratio)**：`--ar 3:4`（肖像/写真首选）或 `--ar 16:9`（电影画幅）
- **画质风格**：`--style raw`（Midjourney 专属，防 AI 塑料假感）
- **采样器 (SD / Flux)**：DPM++ 2M Karras / Euler
- **步数 (Steps)**：30 ~ 40 步
- **CFG Scale**：5.0 ~ 7.0

### 2. 通用负向提示词 (Negative Prompt for SD / WebUI)
```text
deformed hands, missing fingers, extra digits, plastic skin, oversaturated, 3d render, cartoon, anime, low resolution, blurry, bad anatomy, distorted face, extra limbs, ugly, disfigured, bad lighting, watermark, signature
```
