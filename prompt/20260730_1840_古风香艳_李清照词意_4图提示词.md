## 🎬 模式二：动态视频场景提示词 (Dynamic Video Prompts)

适用模型：**Sora / Runway Gen-3 / Kling 可灵 / Hailuo 海螺 / Volcengine Seedance**

### 视频镜头 A · 湿纱微喘 (Slow Push-in Close-up) [🟡 中高风险]
- **运镜语法**：`Slow motion tracking push-in shot`（慢动作跟随推近特写）
- **镜头时长**：5 秒 / 60fps
- **英文 Video Prompt**：
  ```text
  [Slow motion close-up push-in shot]. Li Qingzhao anchor lying on a woven bamboo mat inside a dimly lit Song Dynasty chamber. Her translucent white silk robe moves gently with her rapid shallow breathing, clinging tightly to her glistening wet porcelain skin. Dappled flickering candlelight shifts dynamically across her flushed cheeks. Her cherry lips part slightly in a soft exhale, and a tiny droplet of sweat slowly slides down her collarbone. Song Dynasty Gongbi silk animation style, fluid natural movement, cinematic warm lighting, masterpiece quality, 16:9.
  ```
- **中文镜头解析**：慢特写推近。佳人仰卧于古色古香的禅房竹席上。薄如蝉翼的白丝罗衫随着急促浅快的呼吸微微起伏，紧紧贴合在湿润发亮的肌肤上。晃动的烛光在绯红脸庞上流转，嘴唇微张吐出轻喘，汗珠沿锁骨滑落。

### 视频镜头 B · 影子与水波动势 (Paper Lattice Shadow Pan) [🟢 绝对安全级]
- **运镜语法**：`Slow lateral pan shot`（慢速横向摇镜观影）
- **镜头时长**：5 秒
- **英文 Video Prompt**：
  ```text
  [Slow lateral pan shot]. Inside a traditional Chinese tea pavilion at rainy night, a brass basin filled with water reflects shattered golden ripples under candle light. The camera slowly pans to the translucent paper lattice window, showing the large, soft rhythmic shadows of an embracing couple swaying gracefully inside. Raindrops falling outside on green lotus leaves in slow motion. Traditional watercolor aesthetic, poetic atmosphere, 16:9.
  ```
- **中文镜头解析**：慢速横向摇镜。雨夜茶轩内，铜盆水面在烛光下折射出破碎的金涟漪。镜头摇向透光的纸窗，窗上投影出室内一对情侣优雅缠绵起伏的巨大动感阴影，窗外慢动作滴落雨水于荷叶之上。极具东方隐喻的美学风致。
